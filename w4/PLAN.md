# Workshop 4 — Accessibility and the ethics of AI

**Tue Nov 24 · single session (Thanksgiving cancels Thursday) · depends on the Nov 17 discussion (Ch. 4–5).**
Outline only. Released early with W3 (C8) so students have more time. M4 = who the tool leaves out.

**The idea.** Every rule set has a default learner, the one it was written for. Accessibility is the question *aligned to whom?* Ethics asks what the tool does to the learners it reaches: their data, their trust, their grade.

| Min | Block | What happens |
|---:|---|---|
| 0–5 | **The deal** | Recap: one need each student flagged on Nov 17. |
| 5–12 | **Watch** | One tool, three learners: read by a screen reader, at two grade levels lower, and by a student with no device at home. Then an AI "simplify" that only shortens (the Week 14 Predict → Verify move). |
| 12–32 | **Be the learner it leaves out** | Partner plays a learner the tool wasn't written for: an English learner, a screen-reader user, someone reading two levels below. Run the **accessibility audit**: plain language and reading level, alt text, keyboard-only use, colour contrast, captions on any video, output length. |
| 32–42 | **What this changes for your tool: ethics** | Four checks: **data** (no student names or records go in; the confidentiality rule for professional tools), **disclosure** (the tool says it is AI), **honesty** (it never claims to be the teacher or to grade), **equity** (who is the default learner in your LEARNER block?). |
| 42–55 | **Studio** | Make at least two changes. For each: what need it meets, and how you confirmed it helps. "The AI said it's more accessible" doesn't count. Add the M4 block to `SKILL.md`: who it leaves out, and what it does about it. |
| 55–60 | **Exit card + what is due** | What is due with M4: the skill folder, plus the HTML file (static tools) or a share link (chatbots). Andy publishes Nov 30 for the Dec 1 Showcase (C8). |
