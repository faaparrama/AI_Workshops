# Where Does Confidence Come From? — knowledge.md

## Standard

CASEL, Self-awareness: "The abilities to understand one's own emotions, thoughts, and values and how they influence behavior across contexts." One of its examples: experiencing self-efficacy.
Source: CASEL. *What is the CASEL framework?* https://casel.org/fundamentals-of-sel/what-is-the-casel-framework/

## Ideas, in order

Each one is a sentence a learner can get wrong.

1. Self-efficacy is the belief that you can do one specific task. It is narrower than feeling good about yourself.
2. Mastery experience is succeeding at the task yourself. It is the strongest source.
3. Vicarious experience is watching someone **like you** succeed. Someone very different counts for less.
4. Verbal persuasion is someone you trust telling you that you can. It helps, and it fades if the next try goes badly.
5. Physiological and emotional states are how you read your own body. A racing heart can read as "I'm not ready."
