# Consequence Detective — scenarios.md

Ten scenes, in this order.
Answer and the one-line "why" follow each.

1.  **Mila** turns in her homework on time.
    Ms. Reed thanks her in front of the class.
    Mila turns it in on time all week.
    Answer: Positive reinforcement.
    Why: Praise was added, and on-time homework went up.

2.  **Mr. Cole** tells the class that anyone with an A average skips the final exam.
    Students study harder.
    Answer: Negative reinforcement.
    Why: The exam is taken away, and studying went up.

3.  **Carlie** talks during a test.
    Her teacher hands her an extra page of problems.
    Carlie stops talking during tests.
    Answer: Presentation punishment.
    Why: Extra work was added, and talking went down.

4.  **Kayla** throws paper.
    She loses five minutes of recess.
    The paper throwing stops.
    Answer: Removal punishment.
    Why: Recess was taken away, and throwing went down.

5.  **Maria** dreads the teacher's long lectures.
    Each time she asks a question, the lecture stops for a discussion.
    Maria asks more questions.
    Answer: Negative reinforcement.
    Why: The lecture was taken away, and questions went up.

6.  **Michael** gets a sticker each time he reads for 20 minutes.
    He reads more each week.
    Answer: Positive reinforcement.
    Why: A sticker was added, and reading went up.

7.  **Sam** curses in class.
    The teacher calls home that evening.
    Sam stops cursing.
    Answer: Presentation punishment.
    Why: A call home was added, and cursing went down.

8.  **Maddie** pushes in line.
    She loses her turn as line leader.
    The pushing stops.
    Answer: Removal punishment.
    Why: Line leader was taken away, and pushing went down.

9.  **Ms. Ortiz** lets students who stay on task skip the end-of-day cleanup.
    Time on task goes up.
    Answer: Negative reinforcement.
    Why: The chore was taken away, and on-task behavior went up.

10. **Leo** answers a question.
    His teacher says "No, that's wrong" in front of everyone.
    Leo stops raising his hand.
    Answer: Presentation punishment.
    Why: Public correction was added, and hand-raising went down.
    The teacher did not mean to punish.
    The effect decides.
