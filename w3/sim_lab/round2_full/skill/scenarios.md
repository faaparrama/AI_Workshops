# Consequence Detective — scenarios.md

Ten scenes, in this order. Answer and the one-line "why" follow each.

1. **Jamal** turns in his homework on time. Ms. Reed thanks him in front of the class. Jamal turns it in on time all week.
   Answer: Positive reinforcement. Why: Praise was added, and on-time homework went up.

2. **Mr. Cole** tells the class that anyone with an A average skips the final exam. Students study harder.
   Answer: Negative reinforcement. Why: The exam is taken away, and studying went up.

3. **Dev** talks during a test. His teacher hands him an extra page of problems. Dev stops talking during tests.
   Answer: Presentation punishment. Why: Extra work was added, and talking went down.

4. **Kayla** throws paper. She loses five minutes of recess. The paper throwing stops.
   Answer: Removal punishment. Why: Recess was taken away, and throwing went down.

5. **Maria** dreads the teacher's long lectures. Each time she asks a question, the lecture stops for a discussion. Maria asks more questions.
   Answer: Negative reinforcement. Why: The lecture was taken away, and questions went up.

6. **Ben** gets a sticker each time he reads for 20 minutes. He reads more each week.
   Answer: Positive reinforcement. Why: A sticker was added, and reading went up.

7. **Sam** curses in class. The teacher calls home that evening. Sam stops cursing.
   Answer: Presentation punishment. Why: A call home was added, and cursing went down.

8. **Tia** pushes in line. She loses her turn as line leader. The pushing stops.
   Answer: Removal punishment. Why: Line leader was taken away, and pushing went down.

9. **Ms. Ortiz** lets students who stay on task skip the end-of-day cleanup. Time on task goes up.
   Answer: Negative reinforcement. Why: The chore was taken away, and on-task behavior went up.

10. **Leo** answers a question. His teacher says "No, that's wrong" in front of everyone. Leo stops raising his hand.
    Answer: Presentation punishment. Why: Public correction was added, and hand-raising went down. The teacher did not mean to punish. The effect decides.
