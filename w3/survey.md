# Workshop 3 survey · for Canvas

Eight questions, about five minutes. Paste into a Canvas survey (ungraded), both sections. Wording below is student-facing; the italic lines are for Andy only.

**Intro text for the quiz:**

> Eight short questions. They decide how your tool gets built: which account, which lane, what goes on the page. Nobody's grade depends on the answers.

---

**2. Open that account now. Can you make a project and upload a file to it?** *(Multiple choice)*

- No

*Ask them to check live, in the room.*

**3. Antigravity was an optional install before class. Did you try it?** *(Multiple choice)*

**4. After today: would you let an agent build your tool, if you read every plan before you say yes?** *(Multiple choice)*

- No. I'll build it without an agent.

*New Nov 12. This is the Delegation question from the 4Ds. "No" is a real answer: those students go to Lane A or a static page built in a chat.*

**5. May your finished tool appear in a public catalog?** *(Multiple choice)*

- Yes, without my name
- No

**6. Do you have a GitHub account?** *(Multiple choice)*

- Yes
- No

**7. Picture your tool on screen. What is the first thing the learner sees? Then upload a sketch, or a screenshot of a site or app that looks close.** *(Essay + file upload)*

A sketch on paper, photographed with your phone, is fine.

**8. Write one sentence about an AI agent with a person in it: who set its goal, and who answers for what it does.** *(Short answer)*

Example: "A company set the agent to win the test, and the people who lowered its limits answer for what it did."
